EuoraCraft Launcher (**ECL**) is a **modern, pluggable** third-party Minecraft launcher built with **Python + Tauri**. With just a little code, you can extend the launcher through its plugin system to meet your personal needs.

It's ready to use out of the box:

- Multi-account login with Yggdrasil authentication
- Isolated multi-version instance management
- A built-in multiplayer lobby with virtual networking
- Internationalization support for users worldwide

A **declarative permission model** keeps the security boundary of plugin execution intact.
